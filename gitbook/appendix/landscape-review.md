# Landscape Review

The ZK-Stables Landscape Review provides a comprehensive technical assessment of the current cross-chain bridge ecosystem, evaluating existing bridge architectures, zero-knowledge proof frameworks, and stablecoin interoperability solutions across EVM, Cardano, and Midnight networks. It surveys the competitive landscape of non-custodial bridges, identifies key design tradeoffs (security vs. throughput, privacy vs. composability, trust assumptions vs. decentralization), and positions the ZK-Stables approach within the broader context of multi-chain stablecoin infrastructure.
